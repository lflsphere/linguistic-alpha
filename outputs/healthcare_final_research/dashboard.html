Model definitions

A uses 16 financial and structural controls. B adds five selected Loughran–McDonald (LM) features. B* adds all nine.

Benchmarks and data

CAR[+2,+20] is adjusted by either the S&P 500 price return or a cap-weighted Healthcare return. The partial 2026 test uses complete windows through June 30.

Test 2024 reports original archived results. Those test outcomes informed strategy and estimator selection in the original experiment. Test 2025 and Test 2026 (Partial) use saved expanding-window predictions.

Benchmarks and label maturity

SPRTRN is the S&P 500 price return. The Healthcare series uses dated eligible members of the original static GICS Healthcare basket and same-day capitalization weights. Both targets sum daily stock-minus-benchmark returns from CRSP session +2 through +20. Annual training retains prior-year labels only if Day +20 closes before the refit cutoff. The prediction convention starts after Day +1 close, when Early CAR is known. The original workflow does not establish real-time availability of every predictor.
