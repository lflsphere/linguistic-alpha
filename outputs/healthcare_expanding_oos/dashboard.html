Label maturity and execution time

A refit starts at the first matched event’s Day +1 close. It uses prior-year calls with a saved target and the Day +20 close strictly before the cutoff. Two legacy training labels retain 18 of 19 paired sessions under the original coverage convention. Their full windows elapsed. Early CAR is known after Day +1 close. This design does not establish real-time transcript or financial input availability.
