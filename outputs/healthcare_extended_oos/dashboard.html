05 / METHODS AND DOWNLOADS

Reproduce the evaluation

Cohorts, training and timing

The 29,698-event input preserves archived identity and existing labels. Models are fitted only on 2,939 native development events per option. No additional clock gate is applied. Early CAR[−1,+1] is known after the Day +1 close and the study does not establish transcript or trading availability at that time.

Model estimators, transformations and features

Market A/B use Ridge α 1 and market B* uses Ridge α 10. Sector A uses Ridge α 1, B uses Elastic Net α 0.05 and L1 ratio 0.7, and B* uses Ridge α 50. All fits use unnormalized development midranks. The selected LM5 and full LM9 follow archived order. hedging_answers and uncertainty_answers coincide under the archived dictionary. See the manifest for exact columns, preprocessing and estimator arguments.

Benchmarks, inference and limitations

Daily stock minus benchmark returns are summed over offsets +2 to +20. Sector returns use original static GICS, dated S&P 500 membership and same-day market-cap weights. Returns use legacy CRSP through 2024 and CIZ from 2025. Paired issuer and moving-block resampling report pointwise intervals. The larger method p-value enters the pooled four-test Holm adjustment. Partial 2026 has only three nominal time blocks. Scores are rank scale. No continuous-return regression error or directional metric is shown.
